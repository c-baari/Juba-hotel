About Us

Get to know JUBA Hotel

Who We Are

JUBA Hotel is a comfortable, modern hotel designed for travelers who want quality without a high price. Our rooms are clean and quiet, our staff is friendly, and our location makes it easy to reach the city's main attractions.

We welcome families, business travelers and tourists from around the world.

Our Story

JUBA HOTEL started as a small family guesthouse with only ten rooms. Thanks to our loyal guests, we slowly grew into the hotel you see today, with a restaurant, a swimming pool and a full service team.

Even as we grew, we kept the same promise: treat every guest like part of the family.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To give every guest a clean, safe and relaxing stay with warm and honest service at a fair price.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and recommended hotel in our city, known for hospitality and quality.
